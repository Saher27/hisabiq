FAQ

Before you use the number

Is this a tax calculator?

No. It is business decision support. Validate VAT and fee assumptions against the actual setup.

Does return rate predict actual refunds?

No. It is an expected-value scenario assumption.

Can I use it for every platform?

Yes as a modeling framework, but replace fee assumptions with actual platform/payment terms.

Saudi VAT and e-commerce calculations should be reconciled with the seller's actual records and official guidance. Platform fees can vary by provider and payment method.
