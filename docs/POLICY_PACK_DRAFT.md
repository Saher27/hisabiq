# HISABIQ Production Policy Pack — Draft

This is an internal pre-launch draft. It must be reviewed against the final business identity, applicable Saudi requirements, payment-provider requirements, and the actual product before live checkout is enabled.

## 1. Product description

HISABIQ provides digital business decision-support tools for e-commerce sellers, beginning with a profit and pricing toolkit.

The calculator and toolkit are modeling tools. They are not tax, accounting, financial, or legal advice.

## 2. Pricing and payment disclosure

Before checkout is activated, the final product price, currency, applicable taxes, payment methods, and fulfillment method must be displayed clearly.

No live payment is enabled in the current prototype.

## 3. Digital delivery

After a successful payment, the customer should receive access to the purchased digital toolkit through a controlled delivery mechanism.

The fulfillment system must record:
- order reference
- payment confirmation
- product/version
- delivery status
- delivery timestamp
- refund status

Do not store unnecessary payment credentials or card data.

## 4. Refund policy — draft

Because the product is a digital product, the final refund terms must be written to match the applicable Saudi e-commerce rules and the actual point at which the customer receives or uses the digital product.

Before activation, the policy must specify:
- eligibility
- request method
- time window
- exclusions, if legally applicable
- processing method
- customer support contact

Do not publish a blanket "no refunds" statement without legal review.

## 5. Privacy policy — draft

The site should collect only data necessary for operation, analytics, customer support, payment, fulfillment, and legally required records.

The final policy must identify:
- data collected
- purpose of collection
- analytics/cookies or equivalent technologies used
- third-party processors
- retention approach
- customer rights and contact route
- security measures
- applicable legal basis/requirements

The free calculator should not transmit raw calculator inputs unless there is a documented need and the privacy disclosure supports it.

## 6. Customer support

Before live checkout, publish a real customer-support contact method and business identity information required for the applicable e-commerce channel.

## 7. Legal/identity gate

Live commercial operation must not be treated as ready merely because the website works.

The applicable Saudi business registration / freelance-document route, business bank account, payment-provider eligibility, and e-commerce compliance requirements must be verified before accepting customer payments.

## 8. Release rule

The policy pack becomes production-final only after:
1. Business identity is established.
2. Payment provider requirements are satisfied.
3. Final price is approved.
4. Final refund policy is approved.
5. Final privacy policy is approved.
6. Customer support channel is active.
7. Fulfillment is tested end-to-end.
