# Organic Creative Pack V1

## Creative angles
- Revenue illusion: revenue is not profit.
- CAC ceiling: calculate the maximum affordable acquisition cost.
- Pricing: work backwards from target contribution margin.
- Arabic: show what remains from a 199 SAR order under explicit assumptions.
- Scenario: test what happens when returns increase.

Publishing rules: no fabricated customer results, fake urgency, guaranteed savings/profit claims, or unsupported platform-fee claims.
